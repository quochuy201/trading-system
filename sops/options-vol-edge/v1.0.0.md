# Options Vol-Edge Strategy — v1.0.0

## Overview

This is a **swing strategy SOP** (multi-day holds, 30–120 DTE options). It **explicitly overrides
the day-trade 15:45 flatten**: `OPERATING_MANUAL.md` Rule 7 states the 15:45 time stop is
"non-negotiable *for day-trade SOPs*" — this is not a day-trade SOP, so positions may be held
overnight and across sessions. The daily loop runs an exit check at **15:30 ET** instead; no
mechanical end-of-day close is applied to open options positions.

`OPERATING_MANUAL.md` is the constitution and wins on every conflict. This SOP defers all
mode/limit/sizing-framework decisions to it and **only ever makes itself stricter, never looser**.

---

## Edge Thesis — Two Engines

The options book runs two complementary engines under one SOP, both gated by the same
equity-regime filter and both governed by `OPERATING_MANUAL.md`:

| | **Engine A — Vol-Edge (income)** | **Engine B — Directional / Big-Fish** |
|---|---|---|
| Edge | Vol mispricing: sell rich (IVR > 75), buy cheap (IVR < 25) | Trend continuation — catch the runner |
| Structures | Bull put / bear call credit spreads + debit verticals | Momentum debit spreads + leashed single-leg longs |
| Profit shape | Capped, high win-rate | Spread: capped 3–8× · single-leg: uncapped |
| Scored on | IVR / IV-HV / put-skew | RS63 + regime strength + setup quality |
| Risk | Defined (spread width or premium) | Defined (spread width or premium) |

Both engines are **defined-risk only** — no naked options. Single-leg longs are the sole uncapped
instrument; they are deliberately leashed (see Sizing).

## Account Tiers

Tiers read **live equity** at the start of each session — compounding is structural, not manual.
The system moves between tiers automatically as equity crosses the thresholds below.

| Tier | Equity | Options? | Spread width | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bridge** | < $3.5k | **OFF** → equity-swing fallback (future spec) | — | Compound up; options auto-enable on cross. |
| **Small** | $3.5k–$10k | **ON** | narrow only ($1–$2.50) | Single-leg ($35–$150) more granular than spreads here. |
| **Standard** | $10k–$25k | **ON** | $5 | Referenced $10k profile. |
| **Pro** | $25k+ | **ON** | $5+ | Above PDT; most diversification room. |

The **Bridge equity-swing strategy** (below the $3.5k gate) is out of scope for this SOP. The
gate and fallback hook are noted; the bridge SOP itself is a separate future specification.

## Phase 1: Scan

### Market Context

**SPY Regime (EMA20 / SMA50 / SMA200)**

Compute each morning before the first scan pass. Three possible classifications:

| Classification | Condition |
|---|---|
| **UPTREND** | `Close > EMA20 > SMA50 > SMA200` AND SMA50 slope > 0 over last 10 bars |
| **DOWNTREND** | `Close < EMA20 < SMA50 < SMA200` AND SMA50 slope < 0 over last 10 bars |
| **NEUTRAL** | All other cases |

**VIX Bands**

| VIX Level | Market Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `< 15` | Compressed vol | Favor single-name elevated IVR opportunities |
| `15–25` | Normal | No adjustment |
| `> 25` | Elevated | Reduce spread widths |
| `> 35` | Spike / stress | No new debit long positions |

**Macro Calendar**

- **Hold new entries** the morning of FOMC / CPI / PPI / NFP until 30 minutes after the release.
- If a market-moving scheduled event (FOMC, CPI, PPI, NFP) lands **inside** the intended expiry window, either widen the spread to absorb expected vol expansion, or wait for the event to pass before entering.

---

### IV & Regime Filter

**Formulas (compute these for every candidate):**

```
IVR      = (IV30 − IV30_52wk_low) / (IV30_52wk_high − IV30_52wk_low) × 100
IV/HV    = IV30 / HV20
RS63     = stock_63d_return − SPY_63d_return
put_skew = IV_OTM_put − IV_equidistant_OTM_call
```

**Engine A Vol Routing (IVR threshold)**

| IVR | Signal | Engine A action |
|---|---|---|
| `> 75` | Rich vol | Sell premium → credit spread |
| `< 25` | Cheap vol | Buy premium → debit spread |
| `25–75` | No vol edge | Engine A **skips**; Engine B may still qualify on momentum |

**Equity Regime Gate**

The option structure must agree with the SPY regime:

- **UPTREND** → RS63 must be > 0; bullish structures only (bull put spread, long call debit spread, long call).
- **DOWNTREND** → RS63 must be < 0; bearish structures only (bear call spread, long put debit spread, long put).
- **NEUTRAL** → Engine A skips (no directional credit). Engine B waits for a confirmed directional regime.

**Put Skew Signal**

`put_skew > 5` specifically favors selling puts (bull put spread) over the symmetric bearish equivalent, independent of the regime-direction gate above.

---

### Liquidity & Earnings Gates

All four sub-checks below are **hard gates** — fail any one and skip the candidate.

**Liquidity**

| Check | Minimum | Preferred |
|---|---|---|
| 20-day average dollar volume | ≥ $20M | — |
| Option open interest (target strike) | ≥ 100 | ≥ 500 |
| Bid-ask spread on the spread | ≤ 20% of mid | tighter |

**Earnings**

- The **confirmed** earnings date must fall **entirely outside** the expiry window, OR the expiry window must **start > 7 calendar days after earnings**.
- **If the earnings date is unavailable or uncertain → skip the candidate. Do not guess.**

## Phase 2: Structure, Strike & Expiry Selection

### Engine A — Vol-Edge Structures

Route by the vol signal and SPY regime determined in Phase 1:

| Vol signal | Regime | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Rich vol (IVR > 75) | UPTREND | **Bull put spread** — sell OTM put, buy lower put |
| Rich vol (IVR > 75) | DOWNTREND | **Bear call spread** — sell OTM call, buy higher call |
| Cheap vol (IVR < 25) | UPTREND or DOWNTREND | **Debit vertical** — buy ATM long leg, sell OTM short leg |

**Credit spread — short-strike delta target**

- Standard entry: **0.20–0.25 delta** on the short strike.
- When IVR > 90: move further OTM to **0.15 delta** (inflated vol gives credit at a safer strike).

**Debit vertical — long-leg and short-leg placement**

- Long leg: ATM, targeting **0.45–0.55 delta**.
- Short leg: approximately **1 expected-move OTM**, where:

```
expected_move = stock_price × IV × √(DTE / 365)
```

**Spread width by tier**

| Tier | Width |
|---|---|
| Small | $1–$2.50 |
| Standard | $5 |
| Pro | $5+ |

---

### Engine B — Directional / Big-Fish Structures

**Momentum debit spread**

Buy ATM, sell a **wider OTM short leg** targeting ~1.5–2 expected-moves out (preserves more
upside than the Engine A debit vertical). DTE: **60–90 days**.

**Single-leg long (call or put)**

DTE: **60–120 days** (blunt theta decay, give the move room to develop).

**IV-aware routing:** Engine B performs this check before committing to a single-leg long:

- If `IVR ≥ 50` (vol hot): **prefer the momentum debit spread** — the short leg sells back
  inflated premium and partially funds the long.
- `IVR < 50`: single-leg long is permitted.

**Engine B entry — continuation setup required**

Engine B REQUIRES one of the following confirmed continuation setups before entry. A move that
has already extended without you does not qualify — per `OPERATING_MANUAL.md` Rule 5:
*"a move that started without you is not your move."* Do not chase extended moves.

Accepted continuation setups (one must be present):

1. **Pullback-to-EMA20 with bullish reversal candle** — price pulled back to the 20-day EMA,
   the most recent bar shows a bullish reversal candle (hammer, engulfing, or pin bar), AND
   session volume on the reversal bar ≥ 1.2× the 20-day average daily volume.

2. **Consolidation breakout with Bollinger Band squeeze** — price has been consolidating in a
   tight range with Bollinger Bands contracted (squeeze condition), then breaks out of the range
   with session volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-day average daily volume.

3. **MACD resumption cross** — MACD line crosses above (bullish) or below (bearish) the signal
   line after a pullback, with the histogram turning in the direction of the trend — confirming
   momentum resumption, not a fresh-start reversal.

If none of these setups is present: **skip the candidate.**

---

### Expiry Rules

These windows apply at **entry** — never open a position that already violates them.

| Structure | DTE window | Hard floor |
|---|---|---|
| Credit spreads (bull put, bear call) | **30–45 DTE** | Never < 21 DTE at entry |
| Debit verticals (Engine A) | **60–90 DTE** | — |
| Momentum debit spreads (Engine B) | **60–90 DTE** | — |
| Single-leg longs (Engine B) | **60–120 DTE** | — |

**Earnings-vs-expiry rule (restatement from Phase 1 — enforced here at structure selection):**

The chosen expiry window must satisfy one of:

- Expiry falls **entirely before** the confirmed earnings date, OR
- The expiry window **starts > 7 calendar days after** earnings.

If neither condition is met with the available expiry dates: **skip the candidate or wait for
the event to pass.** If the earnings date is unavailable or uncertain: **skip. Do not guess.**

## Phase 3: Scoring

**Scope:** Scoring is applied ONLY to candidates that have passed ALL Phase 1 scan gates. Any
component marked "reject" in the rubrics below fails the candidate outright — the score is void
and the candidate is skipped. Do not average around a reject.

### Score scale and grade definitions

Both rubrics map to the same 0–100 scale so that `OPERATING_MANUAL.md`'s mode gates and
conviction-scaled sizing work identically across both engines:

| Grade | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **A+** | ≥ 80 | Required for any trade in DEFENSIVE mode (Manual §1: "A+ setups only, score ≥ 80"). Full conviction sizing. |
| **B+** | 70–79 | Permitted in NORMAL mode. Reduced conviction sizing. |
| **Reject** | < 70 | Do not trade. Candidate is discarded. |

**The score drives three downstream decisions:**
1. **DEFENSIVE mode gate** — Manual §1 blocks new entries unless score ≥ 80.
2. **Conviction-scaled sizing** — per-trade risk% is keyed to grade (see Sizing section).
3. **Research candidate ranking** — higher score = higher priority when multiple candidates compete for the same heat headroom.

---

### Rubric 1 — Vol-Edge Score (Engine A), 0–100

Score Engine A candidates (credit spreads and debit verticals) using this rubric. The nominal
maximum is 105 points; **the reported score is capped at 100**.

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| **IVR magnitude** | **Credit structures (IVR ≥ 75):** IVR ≥ 90 → 30 pts · IVR 80–90 → 22 pts · IVR 75–80 → 15 pts · IVR < 75 → **reject** |
| | **Debit structures (IVR < 25):** IVR ≤ 15 → 30 pts · IVR 15–25 → 20 pts · IVR > 25 → **reject** |
| **IV/HV confirm** | **Credit:** IV/HV > 1.3 → 15 pts · IV/HV 1.1–1.3 → 8 pts · else → 0 pts |
| | **Debit:** IV/HV < 0.7 → 15 pts · IV/HV 0.7–0.85 → 8 pts · else → 0 pts |
| **Regime strength** | Full alignment + positive SMA50 slope → 20 pts · Partial alignment → 10 pts · Misaligned → **reject** |
| **RS63 rank** | Top 5% of universe → 15 pts · Top 10% → 10 pts · Top 20% → 5 pts · Positive (above zero) → 2 pts |
| **Liquidity/spread** | OI > 1000 AND bid-ask spread < 5% of mid → 10 pts · OI > 500 AND spread < 10% → 6 pts · OI > 100 → 3 pts · else → **reject** |
| **Earnings buffer** | Earnings > 14 calendar days outside the expiry window → 10 pts · Earnings 7–14 days outside → 5 pts · Earnings inside the expiry window → **reject** |
| **Put-skew bonus** | Bull put spread with put_skew > 5 → 5 pts · else → 0 pts |

**Nominal max: 30 + 15 + 20 + 15 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 105 points. The reported score is capped at
100.** A score of 100 means "maxed all components plus the bonus"; the cap prevents the bonus
from inflating the grade scale.

**A+ threshold:** ≥ 80. **Reject threshold:** < 70.

---

### Rubric 2 — Momentum Score (Engine B), 0–100

Score Engine B candidates (momentum debit spreads and single-leg longs) using this rubric.
Maximum is exactly 100 points; no cap needed.

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| **RS63 rank** | Top 3% of universe → 30 pts · Top 5% → 24 pts · Top 10% → 15 pts · Top 20% → 8 pts · Outside top 20% → **reject** |
| **Regime strength** | Full alignment + positive SMA50 slope → 25 pts · Partial alignment → 12 pts · Misaligned → **reject** |
| **Continuation setup quality** | Textbook setup (all criteria cleanly met — see Phase 2 for accepted setups) → 25 pts · Decent setup (setup present but marginal) → 15 pts · No qualifying setup → **reject** |
| **Liquidity** | OI > 1000 AND bid-ask spread < 10% of mid → 10 pts · OI > 500 → 6 pts · OI > 100 → 3 pts · else → **reject** |
| **IV sanity (single-leg)** | IVR < 50 → 10 pts · IVR 50–70 → 5 pts · IVR > 70 → 0 pts (route to debit spread instead) |

**Maximum: 30 + 25 + 25 + 10 + 10 = 100 points.**

**A+ threshold:** ≥ 80. **Reject threshold:** < 70.

> **Note on IV sanity (single-leg):** IVR > 70 does not reject the candidate — it scores 0 on
> this component and the rubric routes the structure to a debit spread (per Phase 2 Engine B
> IV-aware routing). The candidate can still reach A+ grade if the other components are strong.
