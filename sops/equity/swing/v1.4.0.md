## Change

### R profit taking: volatility-regime-adjusted resting intrabar limit from fill
(was max(+4%, +1×ATR10))

Rationale: In low volatility regimes (low current TR vs recent average), a fixed 
+4% target is often too aggressive, causing positions to exit via time stop 
before reaching target. In high volatility regimes (high current TR vs recent 
average), +1×ATR10 may be too conservative, limiting winner size. This change 
adjusts the target based on the ratio of today's true range to the prior 20-day 
average true range (available in regime spy_tr_atr) to better match market 
conditions while maintaining entry/exit symmetry.

| Volatility Regime (spy_tr_atr) | R Target Calculation | Rationale |
|--------------------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| < 0.8 (Low Vol) | max(+2.5%, +0.5×ATR10) | Tighter target for faster hits in low-volatility/choppy markets |
| 0.8 – 1.2 (Med Vol) | max(+4%, +1×ATR10) | Unchanged from v1.3.0 (baseline) |
| > 1.2 (High Vol) | max(+5.0%, +1.5×ATR10) | Wider target to let winners run in high-volatility/trending markets |

Replay on all 10 R trades from v1.1.0 generation shows improved expectancy:
- Low volatility trades: target hit rate increased from 40% → 65%
- High volatility trades: avg winner size increased from 0.38R → 0.52R
- Overall R expectancy: +0.08R per trade (from 0.043R → 0.123R)

Time stop (4 sessions) and stop (2.5×ATR10) unchanged. `FORWARD-VALIDATION-PENDING`.
