## Change

### Engine R stop: 1.5×ATR10 below fill, close-based (was 2.5×ATR10)

Motivation (structural, found in cycle 4): v1.4.0 R targets are 0.5-1.5×ATR10
by regime tier while the stop risked 2.5×ATR10 — reward/risk fixed at 0.2-0.6
whenever the ATR term dominates, requiring 63-83% WR to break even. The R
trade is a fast washout bounce already bounded by the 4-session time stop;
risking 2.5×ATR buys duration the engine never uses.

Replay sweep on all 15 fillable historical R signals (stop width × v1.4.0
targets held fixed; at constant 1% risk, avg R IS the dollar comparison):

| Stop | ALL avg R | Aug-Oct cohort (n=10) | Dec cohort (n=5) | WR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5×ATR10 (v1.4.0) | +0.137 | +0.160 | +0.092 | 73% |
| 2.0×ATR10 | +0.173 | +0.202 | +0.116 | 73% |
| **1.5×ATR10 (this SOP)** | **+0.239** | **+0.282** | **+0.152** | **73%** |

WR unchanged at every width — only BABA ever touched the tighter stop
(-0.24R vs -0.23R time stop, a wash). Winners bounce without closing
1.5×ATR below the washout fill; losers were time-stop losers regardless.
Net effect: smaller R denominator → ~1.67× shares per 1% risk → target
exits earn 0.33-1.00R instead of 0.20-0.60R.

Notes:
- M-engine stop UNCHANGED at 2.5×ATR10 (slow-grinder engine needs room —
  see v1.5.0 rejections).
- Time-stop losses grow in R terms with the tighter stop (ETSY -0.43 →
  -0.71R); net strongly positive on both cohorts.
- Notional cap (10%) will bind more often on low-ATR% names — acceptable,
  conservative.
- Entry limit (0.5×ATR10 below prev close) unchanged: stop sits 2×ATR10
  below the signal close.
