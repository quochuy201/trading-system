# Equity Swing SOP — v1.1.0 (Two-Engine, backtest-calibrated round 1)

**Status:** thresholds `BACKTEST-DERIVED-IN-SAMPLE` from the Oct 27 – Nov 26,
2025 agent-driven run (reports/backtests/2025-10-27-4week-swing-v1.0.0.md) —
**requires out-of-sample validation before live enablement.** Governed by
`OPERATING_MANUAL.md`. Supersedes v1.0.0 (changes listed in the change log;
everything not listed is unchanged from v1.0.0).

**Round-1 lesson (drove every change below):** v1.0.0's Engine M compressed
Bensdorp Sys-1 (200-day horizon) into a 20-session swing — it kept the worst
part (initiating at momentum highs) and lost the best part (riding for months).
All three -1R losses were initiated with SPY ≥ +3% extended over SMA50 and the
name at run highs. For a swing horizon, BOTH engines must buy weakness: M buys
pullbacks within strong trends; R buys panic stretches. Also: the system is
long-only; in correction months (like the test window) the book's suite earns
via SHORT mean-reversion — an Engine S is on the roadmap, undecided.
