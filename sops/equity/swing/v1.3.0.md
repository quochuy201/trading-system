## Change

### R profit taking: resting intrabar limit at **max(+4%, +1×ATR10)** from fill
(was flat +4%)

Rationale: the entry is ATR-scaled (0.5×ATR below close) but the exit was not —
on a 8%-ATR stock, +4% is half a day's range and systematically sells the
bounce's first hour. This restores Bensdorp Sys-5's 1-ATR target and makes
entry/exit symmetric. Replay on all 10 R trades:

| | Flat +4% (actual) | max(4%, 1×ATR) |
|---|---|---|
| R total P&L | +$312 | **+$711** |
| Changed trades | — | ALAB +193→+398 · APLD +95→+198 · COIN +127→+193 · WBD/HUM small gains |
| Losers | unchanged | unchanged (target never reached either way) |

Time stop (4 sessions) and stop (2.5×ATR10) unchanged. `SINGLE-SAMPLE-REPLAY,
FORWARD-VALIDATION-PENDING`.

## Explicit non-changes (tested and REJECTED — do not re-add without new evidence)

1. **M stagnation exit** ("exit if < +0.5R after 10 sessions"): replay turns
   M's +$3,428 into **-$820** — it dumps CAT-type slow starters right before
   their run. The Monitor agent's legacy dead-money rule (5d/+0.5R) MUST NOT
   be applied to Engine M swing positions.
2. **Trail ratchet** (tighten to 1.25×ATR after +2.5R): zero effect on the
   sample (no trade reaches the arm level before exiting); complexity without
