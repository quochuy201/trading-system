## Change

### Engine M: partial scale-out at +2R (was: no profit-taking below trail)

When an M position's **close** reaches fill + 2R (R = 2.5×ATR10 at entry),
sell **50% at the next open**. The remainder keeps riding the existing
v1.2.0 trail (armed at +1R, width 2×ATR10, no breakeven step). Fires at most
once per position. All levels are fill/ATR-relative — no absolute numbers.

Rationale: the documented M leak is giveback in high-ATR names (run 5: STX
peaked +2.17R, captured +0.73R; on a 7.5%-ATR name the 2×ATR10 trail sits
~15% below peak). Banking half at +2R caps that giveback without touching
the 10 of 11 replay trades that never reached +2R.

Replay on the full 11-trade M set (runs 3+4 calibration, run 5 check):
- Calibration (9 trades): identical to baseline (+3.24R) — zero cost.
- Check (STX): +0.81R → **+1.39R**. SOFI unchanged (-1.13R; exits cannot
  save a bad entry).
- Evidence is n=1 on the upside — `FORWARD-VALIDATION-PENDING`, ships
  because downside on the sample is zero and the mechanism directly targets
  the documented leak.

## Runner-fidelity note (affects interpretation of runs 3-5)

Through run 5, `week_runner.py` hardcoded the **v1.1.0** trail profile
(BE @+1R, trail armed @+1.5R) — the v1.2.0 "trail armed at +1R, no BE"
change was validated in replay arithmetic but **never executed by the
runner**. Recorded M exits in runs 3-5 therefore reflect v1.1.0 trailing.
Fixed 2026-06-11: trail arm/width/BE and scale-out are now plan parameters
supplied by the agent from this SOP; the runner carries no strategy numbers.
Run-day is now idempotent (run 5 executed 2025-10-17 twice, firing time
stops a session early).
