## Diagnosis (mechanical replays, in repo)

1. **M entry timing, not exits.** 4×ATR-stop replay: TSLA still -13.4%, INTC
   -9.4% — wider stops don't fix it. Every loser was initiated with SPY ≥ +3%
   extended above SMA50 and the name at run highs; no loser's price was seen
   again within 20 sessions. v1.0.0 compressed Bensdorp Sys-1 (200-day horizon)
   into 20 sessions: kept buy-at-highs, lost ride-for-months.
2. **R washout threshold too shallow.** Both PLTR R losses (only R fills across
   both runs at RSI3 29.6/27.8) lost; every entry/counterfactual at RSI3 ≤ 15
   finished flat-or-green (SHOP, AMD, INTC cf, MRVL cf, AMZN cf).
3. **R fill starvation + flat-limit miscalibration.** ATR-scaled limit
   (0.5×ATR10) converts AMZN no-fill → +5.1% target win; keeps deep limits on
   wild names. Bensdorp's 7%-limit ≈ 1.4×ATR on his 5%-ATR universe — flat 3%
   was internally inconsistent.
4. **Long-only structural gap.** The book's suite survives correction months
   via SHORT mean-reversion (Sys-2/6). This window was near-unwinnable
   long-only — the gates correctly reduced damage (every loss ≤ 1.02R; Friday
   stress halt avoided the worst day) but nothing could earn.
